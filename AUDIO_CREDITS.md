# Audio sources

Version 0.8.0 restores the historical Minecraft / Iron’s Spells sound routing for the original spell catalogue. God-tier and tactical magic use existing Minecraft sound events, orchestrated at runtime. No synthesized replacement sounds are packaged.

The original owner-supplied Explosion, I Am Atomic, Cosmic Beam, Doom Laser and Angel Scream recordings are retained byte for byte. Their source metadata remains in `assets/resonant_arcana/audio/recordings.json`. Rights to those recordings remain with their respective owners; this update adds no recordings.

Existing sound events remain assets of Minecraft and the installed mods; their audio files are not redistributed here.
