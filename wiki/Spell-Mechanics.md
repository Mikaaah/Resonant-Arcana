# Spell mechanics

Spells use Iron’s mana, native cast bars, ranks, cooldowns and school scaling. Tooltips distinguish damage per hit from multi-stage sequences. Equipment and server configuration can change final values.

God-tier rituals have a 10-second base cast, 600 mana cost and four-minute base cooldown. Their marked ground target locks at cast start. Interruption removes the ritual and its sound layers.

Offensive spells protect allies and terrain and respect solid cover, server PvP and scoreboard friendly-fire rules. Team and claim mods may need their own integration.
