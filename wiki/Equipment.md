# Equipment

All nine books use Iron's native spell container and accept normal inscription-table spells.
Equip one in the Curios spellbook slot to activate its attributes. Merely carrying a book grants no perks.
Held, dropped and item-frame views use the supplied 3D models; inventory, hotbar and JEI use the original flat artwork.

| Book | Slots | Added mana | Equipped bonuses |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Runic Echoes | 14 | 450 | +15% Evocation, +15% Ender power, +10% cooldown reduction |
| Phoenix | 12 | 400 | +25% Fire power, +15% Fire resistance, +15% mana regeneration |
| Deathchill | 12 | 425 | +25% Ice power, +15% Eldritch power, +10% cast-time reduction |
| Book of the Dead | 12 | 375 | +20% Eldritch power, +15% summon damage, +10% mana regeneration |
| Mythic Grimoire | 14 | 400 | +10% spell power, +10% cast-time reduction, +10% cooldown reduction |
| Grimoire of Blood | 12 | 400 | +25% Blood power, +15% Blood resistance, +10% cast-time reduction |
| Grimoire of Stars | 12 | 400 | +20% Ender power, +15% Lightning power, +10% cooldown reduction |
| Grimoire of the Forest Spirit | 12 | 375 | +25% Nature power, +15% Nature resistance, +15% mana regeneration |
| Golden Grimoire | 12 | 400 | +25% Holy power, +15% Holy resistance, +10% cooldown reduction |

Percentages are native Iron's attribute modifiers; final values also depend on other equipment and server settings.
The three original books retain their prior stats and recipes.

## New survival recipes

All six use the existing legendary-book recipe layout: `ASA / BNB / ABA`.
`S` is a Nether Star and `N` is Iron's Netherite Spell Book. No optional addon is required.

| Item ID | A (four) | B (three) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `resonant_arcana:book_of_dead` | Echo Shard | Bone |
| `resonant_arcana:mythic_book` | Amethyst Shard | Crying Obsidian |
| `resonant_arcana:book_of_blood` | Ghast Tear | Nether Wart |
| `resonant_arcana:book_of_star` | Ender Pearl | Amethyst Shard |
| `resonant_arcana:book_of_forrest_spirit` | Moss Block | Glow Berries |
| `resonant_arcana:golden_book` | Gold Ingot | Glowstone Dust |

The supplied `forrest` spelling is retained in the registry ID for resource-pack compatibility.
Display text uses the standard Forest spelling.


## Debug archive

The searchable debug wand is a creative testing tool. It exposes the enabled spells and category filters and has no survival recipe. Normal survival casting uses inscribed spellbooks.
