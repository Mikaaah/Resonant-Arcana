# Optional addons

## Alex’s Caves

Install **Alex’s Caves 2.0.10** and its Citadel dependency on **both client and server** to unlock thirty cave arts, including the six God-tier cave rituals. The adapter uses the installed mod’s particles, sounds, models and attack themes. Other versions leave the adapter disabled; the core addon works without Alex’s Caves.

| Cave area | Novice | Master | Cataclysm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Magnetic Caves | Polarity Bolt | EMP Surge | Magnetar Annihilation |
| Primordial Caves | Amber Talon | Primordial Instinct | Lux’s Caldera |
| Toxic Caves | Acid Glob | Tremorzilla Breath | Nuclear Strike |
| Abyssal Chasm | Brine Bubble | Hullbreaker’s Breach | Abyssal Deluge |
| Forlorn Hollows | Desolate Dagger | Forsaken Sonar | Forlorn Reckoning |
| Candy Cavity | Gumball Volley | Frostmint Hex | Candyland Catastrophe |

The cave Cataclysms use **six-second base casts**, **480–500 mana costs** and a single Legendary rank. Individual spell pages list the exact values. Tremorzilla’s beam uses its native scrolling material and animated impact model, without a miniature boss. Green is the default; hold Amber Curiosity offhand for purple retro breath or a Tectonic Shard for orange. All beam muzzles are at torso level. The large beams follow current aim and hit each enemy once. Nuclear Strike marks a location and drops a visual bomb, giving two seconds of warning before a protected blast and native mushroom cloud. No native boss AI or destructive nuclear explosion runs. All offensive spells retain ally protection and solid-cover checks.

Search the spell archive with `alexscaves`, a biome name or a tier; native spell tags include `integrations/alexscaves`, `caves/<biome>` and `tiers/<tier>`. These scrolls use the existing single Spells tab.

## Bosses’Rise

**Bosses’Rise 2.1.2** optionally adds nineteen boss arts using its installed entities, geometry, textures and sounds. Other versions leave that adapter disabled. No third-party boss assets are bundled. Visual adapters cannot run original boss attacks, persist in saves or drop loot.

## Visual effects

AAAParticles remains optional for selected other effects. All twenty God-tier rituals and Doom Beam bypass its generic seals in 1.0.1 and use vanilla and Iron's particles. See [spell presentation](https://github.com/Mikaaah/Resonant-Arcana/wiki/Spell-Presentation).

Alex’s Caves adds **Planetary Polarity, Primordial Sovereign, Tremorzilla Cataclysm, Abyssal World Tide, Forsaken Eclipse and Confection Apocalypse**. Bosses’Rise adds **Kraken’s World Drown, Yeti’s World Heart, Helvar’s Final Edict, Sandworm’s World Devourer and Dragon’s Primordial Inferno**. These eleven arts appear only with their supported optional mod installed.
