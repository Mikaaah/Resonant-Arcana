## Doom Beam and Rho Aias

Doom Beam uses an open emerald focusing shape, interlaced particle streams, travelling compression fronts and a radial impact. Its previous beacon mesh and black charging orb are removed.

Rho Aias retains its rose-violet flower and three shield discs, with small vanilla enchanted-hit glints. The book inventory lighting and spell icon palettes are also corrected in 1.0.1.

God-tier spells and Doom Beam do not use the optional generic AAA seals. Shared particle limits still apply during overlapping casts.
