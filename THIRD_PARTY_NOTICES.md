# Supplied visual assets

- The spell icons come from the user-supplied `spells-20261110T132324Z-1-001.zip`. Original PNG bytes are preserved in `textures/gui/icon_library`. From 1.0.1, rendered spell icons use matching school or special-theme color ramps while retaining the supplied pixel geometry and alpha. Assignments and palettes are recorded in `tools/spell-icon-map.json` and `tools/spell-icon-palettes.json`. No authorship or new license grant is claimed for the supplied images.
- The nine Arcana book models, UV atlases and flat item icons come from the user-supplied `TNK_3D_Book_Collection_1.1.0.zip`. Its README credits the base model to **Iron's 3D Spell books V2.0 by Lil_Pr1nc3**. The Runic Echoes, Phoenix and Deathchill artwork belongs to the existing Arcana collection; the six new book textures were supplied in `books.zip`.
- The collection's FTB Quest Book assets are not included in this mod.

These notices preserve the supplied asset provenance. The repository's MIT license applies to original code; it does not relicense third-party artwork.
